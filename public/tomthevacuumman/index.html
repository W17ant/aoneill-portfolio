Chapter one · Aylestone Road

A family trade, kept in the family.

Dad's been fixing vacuum cleaners longer than most of his customers have been alive. Tom grew up in the workshop and joined him on the bench years back. Between them they've rebuilt, re-bagged, re-belted and re-motored every machine Britain's thrown at a carpet — from the heaviest Kirby to the tiniest handheld.

Aylestone Road has been home for decades. Three generations of Leicester have dropped off broken Henrys with a weak apologetic smile and come back to find them humming like new. Parts for washers, dryers, cookers — anything with a motor and a bad day — went out of that door too.

The building's been sold. Nobody saw it coming, and the shop has to go. The workshop hasn't. So we're loading the bench into a van and bringing it to your doorstep. You still get the same two pairs of hands. You just don't have to drive to Aylestone.
