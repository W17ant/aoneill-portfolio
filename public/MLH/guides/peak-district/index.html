01 / THE ESCAPE

Plan your base

Start with a campsite reservation. Choose an established site near your walking route and check vehicle size and arrival restrictions before travelling.

02 / THE ESCAPE

Take it slowly

Keep your first day gentle: collect your camper, get familiar with its controls and stock up before heading into the hills. Allow plenty of daylight for your first setup.

03 / THE ESCAPE

Explore from camp

Spend the following day walking from camp. Pack waterproofs, check local forecasts and leave gates as you find them. Park only where campervans are permitted.

04 / THE ESCAPE

Leave time for home

On your final morning, leave time to empty waste at an authorised facility, refuel and return for your agreed handover.
