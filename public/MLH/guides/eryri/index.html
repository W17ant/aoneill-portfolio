01 / THE ESCAPE

Settle into North Wales

For a shorter escape from Manchester, give Eryri four days and keep the plan deliberately small. Choose an established campsite near the area you most want to explore, then stay put long enough to settle in. A base around the northern part of the park can work well for a holiday centred on lakes, villages and walks, but check the campsite’s vehicle access and facilities before reserving. Arrive in daylight, learn your way around the camper and save the ambitious outing for another morning. The first night is for a simple meal and the pleasant feeling of having arrived.

02 / THE ESCAPE

Let the weather lead

Let the forecast set the scale of the day. You can enjoy the landscape without making a summit the purpose of your holiday: choose a suitable lower-level walk, stop in a village or spend a quiet hour looking over the water. If you do plan a mountain walk, use current National Park route advice and choose a route that matches everyone’s experience and fitness. Carry the right clothing, navigation and supplies, and be willing to turn back. A clear morning at camp is only one part of the picture. Keep an easier alternative in your plans so changing course feels like a choice.

03 / THE ESCAPE

One place, a whole day

Give one day to a single area rather than driving between several trailheads. Around Llyn Idwal, use official walking and parking information to decide what is suitable for your group before you leave. Elsewhere, a gentle village wander and a picnic can fill an afternoon just as well. Check public transport options from your campsite if you would like to leave the van behind. Bring waterproofs, dry socks and something easy for supper, then leave the evening free. Part of a campervan break is returning to a familiar space after being outside: somewhere to change, warm up and watch the light move across the hills.

04 / THE ESCAPE

Camp with care

The National Park recommends booking an official campsite in advance. Overnight campervan stays in its car parks are not permitted, and a roadside space is not a substitute for a pitch. Confirm arrival times, use designated facilities for waste and respect the people who live and work nearby. On your last day, choose a short outing close to your route home and allow time for packing and the return handover. Four days will not show you every corner of Eryri. That is a good reason to choose the moments you want most, and leave another valley for next time.

Before you go

Check current access, conditions and opening information before travelling. This itinerary is a starting point, with campsites and activities booked separately.
